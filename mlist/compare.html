2. Catalog size vs usable leads

SaleHoo and Worldwide Brands advertise big totals — SaleHoo has historically claimed millions of products and a large supplier catalog, and Worldwide Brands claims a well-vetted directory. Size is easy to advertise. What matters for a dropshipper or FBA seller is different: how many of those leads you can actually contact this month.

Merchants List is deliberately smaller: 2,238 U.S. suppliers, deduped by domain, with 1,093 email hosts that pass an MX check and 1,792 domains that respond to a live HTTP request. A smaller list of working leads outperforms a larger list of unverified names, because outreach is not measured in rows — it is measured in replies.

3. The verification difference

This is the structural difference. Neither SaleHoo nor Worldwide Brands publishes per-lead domain and email verification results — and neither will show you the dead leads they are not flagging. Merchants List is built to be checked before you buy:

  • Every domain — MX record + HTTP reachability test.
  • Every email — MX check on the host.
  • Dead leads flagged, not hidden — 70 dead domains and 50 invalid emails are labeled, and excluded from the top tiers.
  • Reproducible proof — open any sample record, visit the website, send a test email. It either answers or it does not.
